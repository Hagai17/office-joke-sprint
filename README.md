A tiny Python program that writes one office joke to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 200 characters about office life — a focus-time calendar block that gets booked over anyway, by a meeting titled 'Focus Time (Urgent)'. The line was picked by drafting fourteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a real one-line joke that lands, a punchline that surprises and pays off the setup, one tight line, a fresh and specific detail of office life, and clear on first read. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
